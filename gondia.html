An Overview of Gondia

The town of Gondia is very popular among tourists, and this town in located in the Gondia district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. The district of Gondia is mainly famousfor its wildlife and its natural scenery, and you can visit the various national parks that are located in the region in order to merge as one with nature. For this reason, the town of Gondia is famous among families, who flock to the town regularly in order to visit these parks, as the town of Gondia serves as a base for these activities.


Tourist Attractions

Navegaon National Park

Navegaon National Park is located in the southern part of Gondia, and this park can be easily reached by buses. You can spot many species of birds and animals in the national park at Navegaon, including jungle cats, tigers, panthers, palm cavets, jackals and wolfs. You can also lounge around in the rest houses present in the national park.

Nagzira Wild Life Sanctuary

The wild life sanctuary at Nagzira is yet another important tourist destination, and this sanctuary has more than 34 species of mammals, 36 species of reptiles and 166 species of birds. You can spot many animals like jackals, panthers, tigers and wolves in this sanctuary too.
