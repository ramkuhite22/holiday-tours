An Overview of Chandrapur

Chandrapur city is located on south central region of india in Maharashtra State.It is about 150km from Nagpur,which is two and a half hours' journy by rail on Delhi-Madras route via Nagpur.Nearest airport is at Sonegaon(140km)from the city.

Having Super Thermal Power Station, the largest one in India, Chandrapur City is enriched with different minerals. Coal,Timbers,Tendu Leaves and other forest produces are main resources of this region. Summers are very hot and winter are chilly.

Tourist Attractions

Tadobha National Park and Andhari Tiger Project(45km away) are the places of interects around Chandrapur city. Ramala Talav Garden is newly developed place for all ages,especially for kids. Shri Mahakali Temple, Jain Temple at Bhadravati,Buddha Vihar at Ramdegi, and Somnath are famous worship places of this city.

Chaprala Wildlife Sanctuary

Chaprala Wildlife Sanctuary spreads over an area of 134078 sq. km. The main attraction of this sanctuary is Giant Indian Squirrels. Best Time to visit this sanctuary is February to may. Prashand Dham at Chaprala,Chaprala Temple, and Markanda Temple are not far from this sanctuary.

Ancient Caves

There are nine ancient caves dating back to the eighth century in Chandrapur. It is believed that the caves were first carved out somewhere during the 8th century. Buddhist monks later used these caves. A large number of Buddhist sculptures can be seen engraved in these caves.

 

Markanda

This place is located on the bank of Vainganga river. The land gets its name from Sage Markandeya. A cluster of temples exist here housing 'Shiva Linga'. The cluster has approximately 24 temples and the architecture strongly resembles that of the Khajuraho temples.
