An Overview of Yavatmal

Yavatmal is a city that is located in the North Eastern region of the state of Maharashtra in the Yavatmal district. The city is located at an average elevation of around 1459 feet. Yavatmal has been ruled by many different dynasties and different influences can be seen in and around the city.

There are a few interesting places to visit in the area and these include a few temples and many pristine picnic spots. These are a good option for day trips. There are a few villages that are located round the city and these are also good places to visit. A village called Kalamb is located nearby and there is an ancient temple that can be seen here. The temple is located underground and has been dedicated to Chintamani .There is a large water tank that can be seen here as well and this is known as the Ganesh Kunda. Many festivals and fares are celebrated in the town and visiting during this period is a good option.

Yavatmal is home to the Khojochi Mosque and the Jagat Mandir, and these are some of the most visited places in the area. Other tourist places include Sri Kehetra Kalamb, Maharaja Temple and the Datta Mandir.

Prerna Sthal

'Prerna Sthal' was built in the memory of late freedom fighter Jawaharlal Darda by his sons Mr. Vijaybabu & Mr. Rajendrababu Darda. He has done great work for various sections of Maharashtra Government. 'Prerna Sthal' has become one of the peculiar feature of 'Yavatmal'. It covers area of about 80,000 feet , while making the Smritisthal, Vijaybabu wants it to be the symbol of his father. Late Babuji was nature lover, music lover & also follower of humanity by considering this qualities 'Prerna Sthal' has been created. The famous architect 'Chirag Jain' was appointed for the construction of 'Prerna Sthal'. The bhumipujan of 'Prerna Sthal' was done on 15th June 2001. The stones used in the construction of samadhi were brought from 'Savner'. Samadhi is around 20 x 20 in dimensions for this purpose marble stones were brought from 'Makrana'. 'Samadhi' was surrounded by water from all the side and waterfall from the upper side adds to its beauty. This "Samadhi" inaugurated by Chief Minister 'Shri Narayandutt Tivari' on 25th Nov 2002. Visiting hours are from 6 to 8 a.m. in the morning & 5 to 8 p.m. in the evening. In the morning time pleasant music is played in the prerna sthal. On Every 25th Nov the program is arrange in occasion of "Babuji's Punayatithi". All around the 'Prerna Sthal' there is greenery & also tall Sagvan trees. Special attention is paid for cleanliness.

'Prerna Sthal' is visited by great personality by former President A.P.J. Abdual Kalam, President Pratibhatai Patil, Bhairavsih Shekhavat, Shai shri Ravishankarji, Jagatguru Swami Jayendra Sarswati, Kanchi Jain Muni Tarun Sagarji, Ajit Jogi, Narendra Modi, Lalu prasad Yadav, Manohar Joshi, Vilasrao Deshmukha, Sushilkumar Shinde, Narayan Rane, Ashok Chavahan, Flim Star Amitabh Bachhan, Jaya Bachhan etc... Plantation of trees in the vicinity of Prerna Sthal was done by above mention personality. In the natural surrounding "Prerna Sthal" has become great Tourist spot of "Yavatmal City".

Mahadev Mandir, Chouthis Kalam & Azad Maidan

Mahadev Mandir, Chouthis Kalam & Azad Maidan is in the heart of city. Mahadev Mandir is an antique Hemadpanthi 1100yrs old temple of Kedareshvara. Located in the centre of the town the Kedareshvara shrine is of great antiquity, its sabhamandap having been added only in 1915 by public contributions and subscriptions. The sanctuary, the entrance to which is richly ornamented, contains a largelinga symbol and unlike other linga symbols has a hollow pit in the centre. In front there is a nandi installed on a pedestal where from to the left there are niches one of which houses a brass idol of Shankara sheltered by a eleven hooded cobra also of brass. Yavatmal City is sourrended few such temples at Lohara and Mandev. Some says Lohara and Mandev temples were constructed by Asurs (Raksesh). Chouthis Kalam and Azad Maidan is called as choipatti.

Around Yavatmal

Boregaon Dam

Boregaon Dam (Rajhans Paryatan Sthal) is the beautiful picnic place & also famous as lovers point. It is 5 Km far away from Yavatmal City. There are many things for children's entertainment like Swinging, Sea-saw, Slides etc. This place is not only for the children but also for elders as there is also boating facility is available. There is also snacks center

Jam Wadi (Dam)

Jamwadi (Rajhans Paryatan Sthal) is the beautiful family picnic place. It is 12 Km far away from YavatmalCity. This place is very convenient for people because it is on road. There is small temple of God Shiva. There are many things for children's entertainment , like Swinging, Sea-saw, Slides etc. This place is not only for the children but also for elders ,there is also boating facility is available.

 

Kalamb (Lord Ganesh Temple)

Chintamani Ganapati, one of the 21 altars or Peeth of Lord Ganesh is situated here; the idol here is self-formed or swayambhu. This temple is 12 m below the ground. The steps of the temple descend to the Ganesh kund, a water tank. At the far end of this tank is the 4 feet tall idol of Lord Ganesh, with its trunk facing towards the left. Kalamba is an ancient historical place where archaeological excavations have yielded gold coins and idols.

Wani

Situated on the banks of the river Nirguda is the samadhi of Shri Rangnath Swami. Sadguru Paramahansa Jagannath Baba has his holy abode at Nandeshwar and houses the idols of Vishnu and Shiva. Ancient relics have been found during archaeological excavation in the village of Mandar close to the city of Wani.

Pusad

Pusad is a historical place on the banks of the Pus River. Remains of a Shiva temple built during the Wakataka rule have been found here. Other ancient temples that can be seen here include Karuneshwar, Panchalingeshwar, Harakeshwar and Jageshwar. Bellora of Pusad taluka has the majestic temple of Kedarling. The flow of the Pus River seems to have a semblance to the Jyotirling at this place. An ancient temple of Lord Shiva is situated on a hill at Harshi. Nearby is a place that is said to have the awakened presence of the deity of Jagdamba Mata.

Murli

The Sahastrakund waterfall on the Painganga River in Murli village is famous.

Mahur (Renuka Devi Temple)

It is believed that a Yadava King of Devgiri had constructed the temple about 800-900 years ago. Mahur is amongst the four places of pilgrimage having the abodes of the three and a half goddesses that are of major importance in the state of Maharashtra. The other three, respectively are, Kolhapur, Tuljapur and Saptashringi.

Mahur is the abode of Goddess Renuka, mother of Lord Parashuram. Many temples can be seen here, with the strategically important Ramgad Fort. The place is in Kinwat Taluka and it is about 130 km away from Nanded city. Temples of Lord Parashuram and Lord Dattatreya are also constructed in the neighbourhood. There are caves around the surrounding hills, the view being quite breathtaking. Maharashtra Tourism Development Co-operation has its guesthouse built there for pilgrims` progress.

How to Reach

Yavatmal is accessible from cities such as Nagpur, Mumbai and other important cities in the state. Buses and taxis are the main way to reach the place.

Air

The closest international airport is located at Nagpur and this is situated around 126 km from the city of Yavatmal. Nagpur airport receives flights from different places in the country and is well connected to the metros and this is the best option for those who are planning to arrive by air. Taxis are available from the airport and they charge around Rs 7 per km and this is the easiest way to reach the place.

The Rajiv Gandhi International Airport in Hyderbad is situated around 356 km away from the city and this is also a viable option.
