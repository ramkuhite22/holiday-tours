An Overview of Akola

Akola city is located on south central region of india in Maharashtra State.It is about 250km from Nagpur,which is three and a half hours' journy by rail on Delhi-Madras route via Nagpur.Nearest airport is at Sonegaon(200km)from the city. Akola District divided in July 1998 and make a new district Washim.

Important Tourist Attraction

Narnala Fort

Narnala is an ancient Fortress in the hills in the north of Akot, Taluka at a point where a narrow tongue of Akola District, runs a few miles in to the Melghat. The Fortress lies about 12 miles north of Akot, the road passing through Bordi and the deserted village of Shhapur.

The fort covers 392,acrs and the walls, which only approximately keep at the same level, wind about so much in following the shape of the hill that people say the full circuit measures 24 miles. It would certaintly take very many hours to trace out all the building, especially as the walls, through generally in excellent condition, have crumbled in places and enclosure is much overgrown with long gross and bushes. It is said that there were 22 tanks, six of which still hold water all the year,22 gates, and 360 Buruj towers or Bastions.

Mahadev Temple

The Mahadev Temple at Dhargad is a popular place of pilgrimage for thousands of devotees. Every year on the third monday of the holy month of Shravan(month of August) thousands of people walk miles for a Darshan at the temple. All the pilgrims that approaches the temple from several entry points then treks through the mountains and the forts of Narnala, finally exiting at Shahapur Village.
