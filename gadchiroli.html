An Overview of Gadchiroli

Gadchiroli is one of the Forest equiped District of Maharashtra. It is well known for Adiwasi Culture Region in Maharashtra. The peoples living here are called and "Jangal wasi". The Adiwasi culture and forest are most attraction for others. But Now a day's the name of Gadchiroli is going to popularate as a name of Fear. But it is not truth of the last mile. The peoples living here are the most loving nature, good helping mindset and never heart others. Due to poor literacy and unavailability of developments it is not reaches the main streem.

Tourist Attraction

Chaprala

Chaprala Wildlife Sanctuary spreads over an area of 134078 sq. km. The main attraction of this sanctuary is Giant Indian Squirrels. Best Time to visit this sanctuary is February to may. Prashand Dham at Chaprala,Chaprala Temple, and Markanda Temple are nossst far from this sanctuary.

Semana

Semana Hanuman Temple is located at Gandhi Chowk is around 4 km away from the main town of Garhchiroli.

Bhamragad

In the Bhamragad include the Binagonda Waterfall, Bhamragad Bridge on River Parlkota and Dina Irrigation Project in the Regadi village.

Vipagad

Vipagad is located in Taluka Korchi in the Gadchiroli district.

Vairagad Fort

Vairagad Fort is located in Taluka Armori in the Gadchiroli district were built during the reign of King Virat.It was the Capital of King Virat of Gond Community. There is an ancient temple inside the complex, devoted to Lord Bhandereshwar.

Places of Worship

  • Markandeya Temple

    It is located in Chamorshi Tehsil in Gadchiroli district. The shortest route is via Milswali-Sakhri, which is about 183km. Markandi can be also reached by taking Nagpur - Chandrapur - Mul Road.

  • Ekora

    Ekora Temple is situated west of Vairagad Fort near a Lake that has medicinal values. The Gorjai and Pachpande Temples are located near the main temple of Ekora.

  • Bhandereshwar Temple

    Bhandereshwar Temple is situated in the Vairagad village on a mound located near the conflurence of Vainlochna and Khobragadi rivers. The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and it built in the Hemandpanthi architectural style.

  • Panchpande(Religious)

    Panchpandeis situated in Gadchiroli District. It is famous for an old temple that has five carved stones installed by the Pandava brothers.

  • Adishakti Devi Temple

    Adishakti Devi Temple in Vairagad is known for the beautiful anicient idol of Goddess Adishakti Devi,found 1986 during an excavation. This idol has four hands and is famous for the beautiful carving done by the sculptors during the erstwhile era.

  • Gorjai Temple

    Gorjai Temple lies south of the Vairagad Fort and is devoted to Goddess Gorjai. The temple is situted near the conflurence Khobragadi and Vainlochna rivers and has 18 pillars supporting the ceiling.
