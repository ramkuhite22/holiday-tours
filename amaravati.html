An Overview of Amaravati

Amaravati is one of the district of Vidarbha.Amaravati is in right center of the nothern border of the Maharashtra state.Lying at the Tapi basin,the city's main productions are Cotton,Jowar and Tur(Lentil) and Oranges.

Important Tourist Attraction

Ambadevi Temple

Situated in the heart of Amaravati city,it is believed that Shri Krishna abducted Rukmini,from this temple,and married her. Goddess Amba attracts devotees from different parts of Vidarbha.

Satidham Temple

Situated in the heart of the city at Rallies plot, the temple has beautiful idols of Lord Krishna and Radha,Lord Ram and Sita, lord Ganesha,Lord Shiva and Rani Satiji. A Fair As organized on the occasion of Janmashthami every year. Huge devotees attend the fair.

Wadali Talao

Situating 3km away from the Amaravati camp on Chandur Railway Road, it was built for clear fresh water supply to Amaravati camp. There is a small garden with a zoo here. People usually come here during weekends and enjoy boating. This is one of the best places for kids.

Around Amaravati

Melghat Tigar Reserve

It is located in Chikhaldara and Dharni Tahsils of on the Satpuda hill range. It spreads over on area of 1676.93 sq.km. It is ones of the last remaining habitats of Indian Tiger in Maharashtra.

Wan Sanctuary

Situating at Melghat area of amaravati District, it is an extension to the Melghat canctuary on the Southeastern part. This area is rich in floral and Farnal biodiversity.This hilly ragged terrain has tropical dry deciduous forests.


Chikhaldara

This sole hill resort in the Vidarbha region is situated at an attitude of 1118m above the sea level. This is one of Coffee- growing areas in Maharashtra. Chikhaldara is full of deep valleys with full of valvet mist and majestic tree. Having a Wildlife Sanctuary with abundance of wildlife,Viewpoint,Lakes and Waterfall, it is a very popular summer retreat.

Gavilgad Fort

It is located near the hill station of Chikhaldara in Amravati district. The Hindu rulers originally constructed Gavilgad fort. It is located at a height of about 370 ft above sea level (MSL). Historians believe that it is 200-300 years old, although the fort's history is not known till date.
